# LLM Wiki

## Definition

The LLM Wiki is AIWorkflow Studio's company memory.

It is a Markdown-first knowledge base designed for Human Director review,
Obsidian navigation, and future AI staff context retrieval.

## Not A Raw Dump

The LLM Wiki is not a transcript pile.

Raw material should be promoted through:

```text
capture -> AI Librarian draft -> Human Director review -> Wiki record
```

## Record Types

- Inbox
- Research
- Proposals
- Decisions
- Canon
- Lessons
- Rejected
- Concepts
