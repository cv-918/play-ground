# Director Brief

## Definition

A Director Brief is the structured form of a broad Human Director agenda.

It answers:

- what the Director wants
- what constraints are already known
- which departments or staff should advise
- which decisions may be needed
- which WorkOrders or Work Packets may follow

## Non-Authority

A Director Brief does not:

- approve work
- canonize setting
- modify source/data
- create a task by itself
- commit or push

## Typical Flow

```text
Human agenda -> Director Brief -> Advisory Loop -> Decision / WorkOrder
```
