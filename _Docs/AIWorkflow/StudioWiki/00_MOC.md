## Current Sections

- `Inbox/`: raw captures waiting for classification
- `Research/`: external reference and investigation notes
- `Proposals/`: unaccepted ideas and options
- `Decisions/`: Human Director judgments
- `Canon/`: approved truths and official constraints
- `Lessons/`: reusable learnings
- `Rejected/`: rejected directions and reasons
- `Concepts/`: stable vocabulary and system definitions
- `Templates/`: record templates

## Operating Flow

```text
capture -> classify -> summarize -> connect -> Director decision -> promote
```

## Director Review Checklist

Before promoting a record, confirm:

- the source is visible
- the proposed status is clear
- canon vs proposal vs lesson is not mixed
- rejected ideas are kept out of future plans
- downstream worker context can cite the record
